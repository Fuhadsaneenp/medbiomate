Delete your Medbiomate account

Request deletion of your Medbiomate healthcare job-search account and associated personal data here, even if you no longer have the app installed.

This form sends a deletion request. It does not immediately delete an account. We verify account ownership before processing a request. Never provide your password.

What the request covers

Request deletion of your account and associated profile details, uploaded resumes, saved jobs and application activity held by Medbiomate. Data already shared with an employer is controlled by that employer; contact them separately.

If limited information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal obligations, the team will explain the applicable reason and retention period when handling your request. See the Privacy Policy.

Changed your mind?

If you requested deletion in the app and are within the 30-day recovery period, verify and restore your account here. Signing in alone does not cancel deletion.

Another way to contact us

Email info@medbiomate.com from your account email address with the subject “Medbiomate account deletion”. In the mobile app, the deletion option is under Account → Delete account.
