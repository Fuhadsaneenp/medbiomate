Employer logos and content permissions

Updated 29 October 2026

This policy applies to employer profiles, logos and other company materials submitted to Medbiomate’s website and mobile apps.

Permission before submission

Only upload a logo or company material that you own or are authorised by its rights holder to provide. When submitting or claiming an employer profile, you must confirm your authority to represent the organisation and your permission to use its logo and submitted materials.

Limited permission to display

By submitting authorised materials, you grant Medbiomate a non-exclusive permission to store, resize and display those materials on the relevant employer profile, related job listings and associated website and app discovery surfaces. Ownership remains with the rights holder. This does not transfer trademark ownership or allow unrelated endorsements.

Existing directory entries

Employer names and logos may identify the organisation associated with a job or directory entry. A directory listing does not itself establish an affiliation, endorsement or permission from that employer. This policy does not retroactively grant rights to previously collected materials.

Corrections, withdrawal and removal

A rights holder or authorised representative can request correction or removal at info@medbiomate.com or through Contact us. Include the employer name, page URL, the material concerned and information showing your authority. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

We will assess the request, seek verification where needed and remove or restrict material where permission cannot be established. Removing an account and withdrawing permission for an employer’s materials are separate requests; explain which action you need.
