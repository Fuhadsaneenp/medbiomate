The calculations are based on the minimum terms and conditions prescribed under the respective labour laws of each GCC country, including UAE Federal Labour Law No. 33 of 2021 and its amendments, KSA Labour Law, Qatar Labour Law, Kuwait Labour Law, Bahrain Labour Law, and Oman Labour Law.

Any outcome resulting from the use of this Gratuity Calculator should not be construed as final, accurate, or legally binding. Results are indicative only and may not account for individual contract terms, free zone regulations, company policies, or amendments to labour law from time to time.
